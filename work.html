Helio · Series B SaaS

Analytics people
actually open.

+38% activation

The challenge

Helio had a powerful analytics engine and a product only power users could love. New accounts stalled at setup, and the team was shipping features into a funnel that leaked from the first screen.

What we did

We rebuilt onboarding around a single first insight, not a tour. A nine-week sprint covered research, a new information architecture, a component system, and the front-end implementation alongside Helio's engineers.

The outcome

Activation rose 38% in the first quarter after launch, time-to-first-insight fell by 41%, and the design system cut new-feature design time roughly in half.

Fathom · Fintech

Banking that founders
can read in one glance.

4.9★ store rating

The challenge

Fathom's users ran their companies from a spreadsheet and a banking app that spoke accountant. The brief: make cash flow legible to a founder in under ten seconds, without dumbing down the detail.

What we did

We designed a mobile-first product around a single "runway" view, then built the iOS and Android apps on a shared TypeScript core. Security review and app-store submission were handled in-sprint.

The outcome

Onboarding completion improved 62%, the app launched at 4.9 stars, and support tickets about "where is my money" dropped to near zero in the first month.

Arcline · Developer tools

A deploy pipeline
that gets out of the way.

−62% deploy time

The challenge

Arcline's platform team was the bottleneck for forty engineers. Deploys took 34 minutes, failures were opaque, and every new service needed a platform engineer to hand-hold it into production.

What we did

We audited the pipeline, replaced the bespoke scripts with a declarative service template, and rebuilt observability around deploy-level traces. We then ran a migration alongside the platform team, service by service.

The outcome

Median deploy time fell to 13 minutes, change failure rate dropped by a third, and product engineers now ship new services without platform involvement.

Timeline
14 weeks
Team
3 people
Scope
Platform engineering
Stack
Kubernetes, Terraform, Go
