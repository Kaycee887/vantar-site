01

Product
strategy

Find the smallest bet worth making.

Most teams don't have a feature problem — they have a focus problem. We start by interrogating the business goal, the user's real job, and the constraints you can't move. What survives that is a scope you can defend.

You leave with a written point of view, not a deck: the problem, the wedge, the metrics, and the risks.

  • Discovery workshops
  • User interviews
  • Market & competitor scan
  • Opportunity mapping
  • MVP scoping
  • Success metrics
  • Technical feasibility
  • Roadmap & budget
Typical outcome

A decision-ready brief in 5–10 days — and a stop/go recommendation we're willing to put our name on.

02

Interface
design

Make the right thing feel obvious.

We design in flows, not frames. Every screen earns its place against a user goal, and every state — empty, loading, error, success — is considered before engineering begins.

Our output is a system your team can extend without us: tokens, components, patterns, and the reasoning behind each decision.

  • UX architecture
  • Interactive prototypes
  • Visual direction
  • Design system
  • Motion & micro-interaction
  • Accessibility review
  • Usability testing
  • Developer handoff
Typical outcome

A clickable prototype in 2–3 weeks, validated with five real users before a single sprint is committed.

03

Engineering

Ship it, then keep shipping.

We build web and mobile products the way we'd want to inherit them: typed, tested, observable, and documented. No prototype code in production, no résumé-driven architecture.

Our engineers sit in the design room from day one, so the thing you saw in Figma is the thing that reaches your users.

  • Web applications
  • iOS & Android
  • API design
  • Cloud infrastructure
  • CI/CD pipelines
  • AI integration
  • Performance & security
  • Observability
Typical outcome

A production deploy in 6–10 weeks, with monitoring and a runbook your team can actually use.

Questions

Before you ask.

How quickly can you start?

We hold two project slots per quarter. For Q1 2027 we have both open. Discovery can usually begin within two weeks of a signed agreement.

Do you work with existing codebases?

Often. We start with a short technical audit to decide whether to extend, refactor, or rebuild. We'll give you the honest answer even when it costs us the larger project.

Who owns the work?

You do — code, design files, and documentation, from day one. We work in your repositories where possible and never hold your product hostage.

What happens after launch?

Every project includes one month of post-launch support. Beyond that, most clients move to a monthly care plan or bring us back for the next phase.

Do you work remote?

Yes. We're based in Lisbon and Berlin and work across European and US time zones. We meet in person for kickoff and major milestones when it helps.
