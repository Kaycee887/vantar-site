How we work

Four weeks to momentum.

A tight, transparent loop. You see progress every Friday.

  1. 01

    Frame

    One week of deep discovery. We pressure-test the problem, define success, and agree on scope.

  2. 02

    Shape

    Flows, prototypes, and a visual direction. We validate with real users before a line of code.

  3. 03

    Build

    Two-week sprints with working software at the end of each. Weekly demos, shared backlog.

  4. 04

    Launch & learn

    Ship, instrument, iterate. We stay on through the first month of real usage.
